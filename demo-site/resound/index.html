${icon('recycle',15)} The order to build it in

1 · Database and hosting. Supabase and Vercel, deployed on day one even when empty.
2 · Sign-in. Locked to @resoundav.com addresses. Now it's private and real data can go in.
3 · Proposals and inventory, with nothing connected. Prove the workflow matches how you actually price and stock before wiring anything external.
4 · QuickBooks, read-only first — pull the invoice list in and check it matches. Only then let it write.
5 · Drive and Gmail, once the team relies on the core.
6 · The rest — AI drafting, Slack, e-sign, QR — one at a time, each able to fail without taking the app down.

${icon('info',15)} What this replaces

The estimator spreadsheet, the PACKLIST tab, and the manual leftover count all fold into this app.
Nothing you already pay for goes away. QuickBooks stays the books. Drive stays the filing cabinet. Slack stays the team channel. The app is the layer that connects them and stops the same job number being typed into three places.
The honest cost picture: under $60/month in new spend, and the real investment is building it.
